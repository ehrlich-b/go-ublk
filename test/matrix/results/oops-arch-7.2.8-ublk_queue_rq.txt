            DEL_DEV timed out, and the guest could not power off.

On other kernels the same test failed in other ways:
- EBUSY opening /dev/ublkc0: Fedora 6.19/7.2.8, arch-lts 6.18.55, mainline 7.0.14.
- A 10s START_DEV timeout followed by hung tasks: Debian 6.12, mainline 6.10-6.16.
go-ublk now treats Start after Stop as unsupported, and the matrix skips this
test from run m3 on.
